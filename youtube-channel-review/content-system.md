# Content system: consistency, hooks, ideas

## Weekly rhythm

**Target:** 3 Shorts a week, plus 1 long video every 2 weeks. Second year will be busy, so only raise this once it feels easy.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Every day (5 min) | Film 2–3 short clips of whatever is happening. No editing. |
| Sunday | Edit that week's Shorts in one sitting and schedule them in YouTube Studio. |
| Mon / Wed / Fri | Shorts go live. Check Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" to pick the time. |
| Every other weekend | Edit the long video. |

**Motivation rule:** count the week as a win if all 3 Shorts went out, whatever the views. Shorts views swing a lot from one video to the next, so judge after 12 weeks, not 1.

**Idea bank:** keep a "YT ideas" note on your phone. Whenever something funny, annoying or surprising happens, write one line. Also add the replies to your pinned-comment questions.

## Hook checklist (every Short)

1. **The first frame is already moving.** No intro and no "hi guys." Start in the middle of the action.
2. **The text on screen states the hook in the first second.**
3. **Open a loop:** ask a question or create tension that only gets answered at the end, so people watch to the end.
4. **Keep it under 30 seconds.** Cut every pause.
5. **The title repeats the hook in casual Hinglish,** with one word in CAPS.

### Hook formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Expectation vs reality | "MBBS student ka vacation: plan vs reality 😭" |
| What they think vs what I do | "What my family thinks I do in MBBS vs what I actually do" |
| Question to the viewer | "Would you live alone at 18 for MBBS?" |
| Number + stakes | "₹X a month: what living alone in Patna for MBBS really costs" |
| POV | "POV: you're the only 'doctor' at the family function" |
| Relatable dialogue | "Rishtedaar: 'beta, ab toh doctor ban gaye?' 🙂" |
| Before → after | "1 year ago: crying over NEET result. Now:" |

Only use a hook if it's true to your life.

## Ideas: at home during the festive vacation

1. **"Ghar aate hi family ne mujhe DOCTOR bana diya 😭"**: everyone asks you to check their BP or explain their symptoms. Keep it funny and don't give real medical advice on camera.
2. **"Rishtedaar: 'beta, ab toh doctor ban gaye?'"**: questions from relatives at the festival, and your inner reaction.
3. **"MBBS student ka vacation: plan vs reality"**: you brought books home and didn't open them.
4. **"Mom's food after months of flat ka khana 🥹"**: your first home-cooked meal back.
5. **Festival day mini-vlog**: getting ready, lights, family, sweets. Festival content does very well.
6. **"What I eat at home vs in my Patna flat"**: a side-by-side contrast.
7. **"Packing ghar ka khana for Patna 😭"**: mom packing food the day you leave.
8. **"Last day of vacation → back to MBBS"**: the journey back to Patna and the start of second year.

## Ideas: back in Patna (flat life)

1. **"Hostel nahi mila, so I live in a flat in Patna"**: a flat tour (long video). It's searchable, and NEET aspirants heading to NMCH will want to see it.
2. **"My monthly expenses as an MBBS student in Patna"**: rent, food, travel. Use real numbers.
3. **"Living alone at 18–19 for MBBS: is it scary?"** (Mitali's version got 538K)
4. **"5:30 AM morning before college"**: a morning routine mini-vlog.
5. **"Cooking in my flat after college"**: easy, repeatable, and relatable.
6. **"12 AM study + Maggi"**: late-night study mini-vlog.
7. **"First day of second year MBBS"**: a milestone, so don't skip it.
8. **"Flat makeover for second year"**: a before-and-after setup.
