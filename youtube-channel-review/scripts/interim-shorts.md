# Interim Shorts scripts (now → 1st prof result)

These are the Shorts to post until your result comes out. **H** = filmed at home during the vacation, **P** = filmed back in Patna. The 4 already-captioned Shorts are in `shorts-captions.md`, and the expenses Short (H3) is in `monthly-expenses.md`.

**Rules for every Short**
- Start in the middle of the action. The hook text is on screen from frame 1.
- Keep it under 30 seconds. Change the shot or text every 2–3 seconds.
- End on something that loops back to the start, or on a question.
- If a line isn't true to your life, change it. Real moments beat staged ones.
