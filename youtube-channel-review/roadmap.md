# Roadmap: now → July 2027

Built 2026-10-22 from research on YouTube's own guidance, the Bihar MBBS calendar, and Indian MBBS creators. Dates marked *est.* are estimates, so check them before relying on them.

Scripts for every Short in Phases 1–2 (H1–H7 home, P1–P13 Patna) are in `scripts/interim-shorts.md`.

## The facts this plan is built on

- **My 1st prof result will probably take a while.** NMCH's exams are run by **BUHS** (Bihar University of Health Sciences), not AKU. The previous batch sat its 1st prof in Oct 2025 and got results in **Feb 2026, about 4 months later**, and its Phase II classes started only after that. So my result is *est.* **Jan–Feb 2027**, and second year might not fully start until then. Check buhs.ac.in and college notices.
- **New juniors are arriving now.** NMCH went from 150 to **200 MBBS seats** in 2026, and the new batch's session was expected to start around October. They are the people most likely to search for an NMCH senior's advice right now.
- **Festival vacation:** Diwali **Sun 8 Nov**, Bhai Dooj **10–11 Nov**, Chhath **13–16 Nov** (Nahay Khay 13, Kharna 14, Sandhya Arghya 15, Usha Arghya 16).
- **NEET 2027** (*est.*): applications Feb–Mar, exam around **2 May**, result around **mid-June 2027**. That's the 2-year anniversary of my NEET result video.
- **What YouTube itself says** (not just guides):
  - Inactive subscribers don't block recommendations. *(Reported, not fully confirmed.)*
  - **Don't delete old videos.** Unlist them instead.
  - One weak video doesn't hurt the channel.
  - The "related video" link on a Short should point to a closely matching long video, with a call to action in the last 5 seconds.
  - Hype (viewers boosting long videos) works in India, but only once I'm in YPP.
- **The next milestone is YPP lower tier:** **500 subs ✅** + 3 uploads in 90 days + **3,000 watch hours (12 months) or 3M Shorts views (90 days)**. That unlocks memberships, Supers and Hype. The full tier, with ad revenue, needs 1,000 subs + 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views.

## Phase 3: Result day (*est.* Jan–Feb 2027)

This is the same playbook that got 485K views: **film the real moment and post within hours.**

**Prepare now:**
- Turn on result notifications (buhs.ac.in, college groups).
- Make a thumbnail template with an empty space for the result.
- Decide where you'll check it and who'll be with you (family on a call? friends?).
- Keep your phone charged and set up to record.

**Film:**
- Before: nervous, refreshing the page.
- The moment itself.
- Reactions: calling family, friends.

**Post:**
- A **Short within 4 hours:** "MY 1ST YEAR MBBS RESULT 😭 #mbbs #nmch #buhs"
- A **long video within 1–2 days:** "My 1st Prof MBBS Result: Honest Reaction + Marks"
- **Pin a comment on the old 485K NEET video:** "1 year later, here's my MBBS result."

**If the result is bad:** you don't have to post. If you do, an honest "I failed / got a supply, here's what I'll do" video can help a lot of students, but it's your choice and there's no pressure.
