# 3D Realistic Explainer Style ("Zack D. Films" look)

Learned from 3 reference shorts (720x1280, 30–54 s): water-in-ear hack, the
$100 store-theft riddle, and a shoebox phone projector DIY.

## Format
- Vertical 9:16, 30–55 s, one idea per video, no intro/outro, no logo sting.
- Fast voice-over (neutral, confident American narrator), one continuous
  sentence chain. Starts mid-action on the first word.
- Captions: 1–3 words at a time, white bold sans (Montserrat/Arial-Black
  feel) with soft dark drop shadow, centred at ~78% of frame height. No
  background box, no colour highlight. Caption changes on every phrase.

## Story structure (3 templates)
1. **Life hack / body science** (ear video)
   Hook situation ("If you fall into a pool and water gets stuck in your
   ear") → do-this steps ("tilt that ear… press your palm… lift") →
   cut-away anatomy cross-section showing WHY (suction, gravity 9.8 m/s,
   pressure) → payoff shot (water drips out, relief face).
2. **Riddle / puzzle** (store video)
   Fast story setup in 3–5 beats → question to viewer ("How much did the
   store actually lose?") with a big 3D "?" → the WRONG popular answer
   worked out on a chalkboard → a second character interrupts → correct
   answer + reasoning → smug closing character beat.
3. **DIY build** (projector video)
   No narration needed; pure process: cut part → measure/mark → cut hole →
   glue → build stand → prep phone (brightness up, rotation lock, flip
   image) → assemble → lights off → reveal: big image on the wall, character
   enjoying it. The reveal is the reward.

Rules: every line of narration is shown literally on screen; never talk
about something the viewer cannot see. Payoff in the last 3–5 s. Loopable
ending (end frame often echoes the opening scene).

## Visual language
- Photoreal-ish 3D (Blender/Unreal style) with slightly stylised humans:
  big expressive eyes, freckles, pores, soft subsurface skin. Characters are
  generic (kid girl with space buns, guy in orange tee, shopkeeper in green
  apron, suited "professor" with moustache).
- Clean, saturated, bright palette: cyan/sky-blue backgrounds dominate,
  with one warm accent (pink tank top, orange shirt, red candy lids).
- Studio-blue infinite backdrops for "object demo" shots; real environments
  (pool, convenience store, workshop desk) for story shots.
- Extreme close-ups and macro shots: hands, ears, cash drawer, glue tip.
  Shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh.
- **X-ray / cross-section inserts**: anatomy cut-aways on a flat cyan
  background (ear canal with yellow fat tissue, bone texture, coloured
  liquid), dissolving out of the skin.
- **Diegetic 3D graphics**: floating +$100 (green) / -$100 (red) labels,
  glowing outlines (yellow/green rim highlight on the object being talked
  about), cyan arrows showing direction, "90°" angle markers, giant 3D
  question marks, coin with down-trend arrow, numbers like "9.8 m/s".
- Analogies rendered literally (a plunger appears when narrator says
  "like a plunger").
- Branded props with the channel name (ZACK D. MART, ZACK D. SHOES, tape
  saying ZACK D. FILMS) instead of real brands.

## Camera & editing
- A new shot every ~0.8–1.5 s. Almost no hard cuts: transitions are
  whip-pans, motion-blur smashes, push-ins through an object, and match
  moves (camera flies from face into ear cross-section).
- Constant camera motion: slow dolly-in, orbit, handheld-like drift. Never a
  static frame.
- Speed ramps: fast move → slow on the key moment (bill entering pocket,
  water drop falling).
- Chalkboard scenes: chalk writing animates in sync with words, small
  picture cards stuck under each number.

## Sound
- Voice-over on top, light upbeat/curious BGM low in the mix.
- Foley on every action: splash, register "ka-ching", saw, glue, light
  switch, drip, whoosh on each transition, pop on each graphic.

## Prompting it (per shot, for AI video/3D tools)
"Hyper-detailed 3D animated render, Pixar-meets-photoreal, vertical 9:16,
[subject + action], bright cyan studio background / [environment], soft
cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, slow push-in camera, glossy
materials, subsurface skin, freckles, expressive eyes"
For inserts: "medical 3D cross-section of [organ], flat cyan background,
clean labelled-free anatomy, translucent liquid simulation".
