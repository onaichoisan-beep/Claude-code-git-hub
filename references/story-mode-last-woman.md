## What makes it work

**Hook (0-11 s).** An impossible premise + "you" in the first sentence ("...except you"), then the stakes immediately (a huge
number, 4 billion), then a threat in 5 words ("Not all of them want to help."). Three lines, three escalations, all before 11 s.

**Story engine.** After the hook "you" becomes a named character (Anna) so the viewer follows a person. The middle is a
**numbered list of rules** (Rule 1 / 2 / 3): chapters the viewer wants to complete. Then a **chase** (escalation), an
**escape** ("just in time"), **false safety** (Day 3, Day 7, sleep), and a **twist that reverses the premise** ("The last
woman has been found." But she's the last woman. "So, who did they find?"). It ends on an unanswered question + an ominous
door = comments ("who was it??") and rewatches.

**Writing.** Fragments of 1-4 words ("Cap. Hood." "Food. A stove. Silence."). Present tense. Concrete actions, not
explanations. Every sentence is something you can *see*. One sentence = one shot.

**Pacing.** A new shot every 1-2 s; inserts of hands/objects for every verb (SNAP / OFF / LEFT BEHIND); time cards
(DAY 1 -> 3 -> 7 calendar flips) to jump time; a persistent HUD (top-left checklist of the rules done, and a top tag
"DAY 1 · 08:12 · TRAIN") that shows progress the whole video.

**Captions.** Big bold words, built up word by word as they are spoken (ABOUT -> ABOUT 4 BILLION), lower-middle, max 2 lines,
red = danger, gold = key, green = safe.

**Sound.** No beat. A low cinematic drone/pad bed ~11 dB under the voice, never silent; literal foley for every action
(SIM snap, button click, scissors, footsteps, train doors, radio static + voice, kettle); a whoosh/impact on key words;
the chase builds (footsteps + rising); the end gets quieter and more tense before the last line.
