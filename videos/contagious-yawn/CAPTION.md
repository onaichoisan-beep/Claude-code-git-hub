Don't Yawn 🥱🎧

Why do yawns spread? Scientists still don't fully know.

About half of adults yawn after seeing someone else yawn, and even reading or thinking about yawning can set it off. Dogs can catch yawns from people. It spreads more between close friends and family than between strangers. Kids usually don't catch yawns until around age 4 or 5. Why we yawn at all is still debated; one idea is that it helps cool the brain.

So... did you yawn? Be honest 👇

Sources: Provine (1986, 2005); Joly-Mascheroni et al. (2008) Biology Letters; Norscia & Palagi (2011) PLoS ONE; Gallup & Gallup (2007)
