The Jellyfish That Ages Backwards 🪼🔁🎧

Can an animal live forever? This one might.

Turritopsis dohrnii is only about 4.5 mm wide. When it's injured, starving or old, it sinks, shrinks into a blob and turns back into a polyp: the baby stage. Then it grows up all over again. It can do this again and again, which is why scientists call it "biologically immortal". It has even spread to oceans around the world. The catch? In the wild most of them still get eaten or die of disease.
