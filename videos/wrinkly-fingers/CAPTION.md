Your Fingers Wrinkle on Purpose 🛁🎧

Why do your fingers wrinkle in the bath? It's not water soaking in.

Only fingers and toes do it, and it takes about 5 minutes. Your nerves send a signal and the blood vessels shrink on purpose. If the nerve is cut, the finger doesn't wrinkle at all, and doctors used this as a nerve test. Why do we have it? One theory: rain treads for a better grip on wet things, though studies are still mixed.
