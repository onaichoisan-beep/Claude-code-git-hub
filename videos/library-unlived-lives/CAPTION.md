The Library of Unlived Lives 🎧

What if every choice you didn't make became a book?

Somewhere there's a library with a book for every life you didn't live: the one where you said yes, the one where you took the other road, the one where you never met them. People come looking for a happier ending. But in every single book, the other you is reading about you, wishing they were you. And a brand-new book just appeared: the life where you scrolled past this video. You didn't. Good choice.

An original fable. 📚
