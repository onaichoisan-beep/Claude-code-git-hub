This Rat Shouldn't Exist 🐀🎧

Ugly? Yes. But this rat breaks 6 rules of biology.

The naked mole-rat lives up to 37 years (other rats: about 3), almost never gets cancer, doesn't feel acid burn, survived 18 minutes without oxygen, and lives in colonies with one queen like bees. The strangest part: its risk of dying doesn't go up as it gets older. Scientists study it for anti-aging clues.

Ugly or immortal? 👇

Sources: Park et al. (2017) Science; Ruby, Smith & Buffenstein (2018) eLife; Buffenstein (2008)
