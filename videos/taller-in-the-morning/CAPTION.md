You Shrink Every Day 🎧

Are you shorter at night? Yes, by about 1 cm.

The soft discs between your spine bones are full of water. All day, gravity squeezes some of it out, so by bedtime you've lost about a centimetre (roughly 1% of your height). Lie down, and the discs soak it back up overnight. In space, with no gravity pressing down, astronauts grow up to 5 cm taller, then shrink back on Earth.

Sources: Tyrrell, Reilly & Troup (1985), Spine, "Circadian variation in stature and the effects of spinal loading"; NASA, astronaut spinal elongation in microgravity
