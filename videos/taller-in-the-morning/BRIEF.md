## Facts

- Height varies ~1% over the day (about 1 cm, sometimes more) as intervertebral discs lose fluid under load and rehydrate lying down
  (Tyrrell, Reilly & Troup 1985, Spine; Reilly et al. 1984).
- Astronauts grow up to ~3% (about 5 cm / 2 in) in microgravity and return to normal on Earth (NASA).
