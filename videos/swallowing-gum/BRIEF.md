## Intent

Bright pixel-art science short in the user's established style (see videos/snake-bite-venom):
one continuous camera journey, only key facts as small text in the middle, sound effects only,
complete story start to finish, looping ending. Built autonomously.

Story: kid blows a bubble, it pops, gulp → x-ray body → oesophagus muscle waves squeeze it down →
stomach acid washes out sugar and flavour but can't digest the gum base → it rides along with food
through the gut (day counter) → out in a few days → "7 years? Myth" → "Just don't swallow a lot at once" → loop.

## Notes

- Gum base is indigestible but passes normally within days; rare blockages are reported only when large
  amounts are swallowed (mostly young children), hence the closing line.
