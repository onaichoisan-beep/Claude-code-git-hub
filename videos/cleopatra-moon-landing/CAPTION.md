Cleopatra and the Moon Landing 🎧

Was Cleopatra closer in time to the Moon landing or to the pyramids? The Moon landing.

The Great Pyramid of Giza was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra died in 30 BC, about 2,500 years later. Apollo 11 landed on the Moon in 1969, about 2,000 years after her. To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient monuments. And while the pyramid was being built, the last woolly mammoths were still alive on Wrangel Island in the Arctic.

Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Pyramids of Giza" and "Cleopatra"; NASA, Apollo 11; Vartanyan et al. (1993), Nature, "Holocene dwarf mammoths from Wrangel Island"
