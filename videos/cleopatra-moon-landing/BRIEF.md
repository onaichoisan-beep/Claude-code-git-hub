## Intent

Owner asked for this "insane fact" with NO narration: only small text in the middle and loud open-source sound effects.
Sharp HD pixel art, outlined characters (Cleopatra, the astronaut, the mammoth).

## Sound

No narration. OpenGameArt CC0 (slams, stones, sci-fi beeps, gong, roar, "ooh"), jsfxr, HyperFrames library,
sfxlib (impact, braam, taiko, riser, rewind) and foley (wind, ticks, squawks).

## Facts

- Great Pyramid of Giza: completed c. 2560 BC (reign of Khufu). Cleopatra VII died 30 BC. Apollo 11 landed 1969.
  2560 − 30 ≈ 2,530 years; 30 BC → AD 1969 ≈ 1,998 years.
- The last woolly mammoths survived on Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago (~2000 BC) (Vartanyan et al. 1993, Nature).
