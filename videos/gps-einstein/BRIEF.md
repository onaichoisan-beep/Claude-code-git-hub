## Facts

Ashby (2003), Living Reviews in Relativity 6:1, "Relativity in the Global Positioning System": orbit clocks run fast by
~45.7 µs/day (weaker gravity) and slow by ~7.1 µs/day (orbital speed), net ~+38.6 µs/day. 38 µs × speed of light ≈ 11.4 km,
so uncorrected range errors would grow by ~10 km per day. The satellite clocks' 10.23 MHz frequency is offset to
10.22999999543 MHz before launch.
