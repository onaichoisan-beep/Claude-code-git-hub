Who Keeps Breaking My Aquarium? 🦐🔍🎧

Smashed crabs. Cracked shells. A click at night. Then the glass cracked.

Suspects: the eel, the octopus, the pufferfish. The real culprit? A 10 cm mantis shrimp. It punches at about 80 km/h, so fast the water around its club bursts into bubbles, and it can crack aquarium glass. It has up to 16 kinds of colour sensors (we have 3)... yet it's worse at telling colours apart than we are. (The aquarium is fiction; the shrimp is real.)

Did you guess it? 👇

Sources: Patek, Korff & Caldwell (2004) Nature; Thoen et al. (2014) Science
