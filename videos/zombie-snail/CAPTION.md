Why would a snail walk into the sun for a bird to see?

A parasite called Leucochloridium lives inside it. It fills the snail's eye-stalks with pulsing, caterpillar-like sacs, and infected snails spend more time out in the open. A bird pecks the "caterpillar", the worm grows inside the bird, its eggs come out in the poop... and a snail eats them. Repeat.
