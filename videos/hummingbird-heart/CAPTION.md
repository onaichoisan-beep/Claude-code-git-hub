The Heart That Beats 20 Times a Second 🐦❤️🎧

How fast can a heart beat? A hummingbird's can hit about 1,200 beats a minute.

Yours beats around 70 times a minute. A hummingbird's peaks around 1,200, about 20 beats every second, while its wings flap roughly 53 times a second and it visits 1,000+ flowers a day. That's so expensive that a cold night with no food could kill it. So it goes into torpor: its heart drops to about 50 beats a minute and its body cools way down (one Andean species was measured at just 3.3°C). It looks dead. At sunrise it shivers, warms up and reboots back to full speed. ⚡
