The King Who Lost to a Grain of Rice ♟️🍚

How can a few grains of rice bankrupt a king? Doubling.

Legend says the inventor of chess asked the king for just rice: 1 grain on the first square, then double it on every square. The king laughed and agreed. Square 10: 512 grains. Square 20: half a million. Square 32: over 2 billion grains, about 50 tonnes. And square 64 alone? 9,223,372,036,854,775,808 grains. The whole board adds up to about 18.4 quintillion grains, more rice than humans have ever grown. The king could never pay. Checkmate.
