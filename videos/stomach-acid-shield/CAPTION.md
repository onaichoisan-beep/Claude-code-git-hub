Why Doesn't Your Stomach Digest Itself? 🫃🧪🎧

Your stomach is full of acid strong enough to digest meat. So why doesn't it eat itself? It has a shield.

Stomach acid sits at about pH 1.5-3.5 and, together with the enzyme pepsin, breaks down protein. The stomach wall is protected by a layer of mucus loaded with bicarbonate: acid that reaches it gets neutralised, so right at the lining the pH stays close to neutral. On top of that, the surface cells of the lining are replaced every few days, repairing everyday damage. When this defence fails, the acid eats into the wall and you get an ulcer. The most common causes are the bacterium H. pylori and regular use of painkillers like ibuprofen and aspirin (NSAIDs). Spicy food doesn't cause ulcers, though it can make the pain worse.

Your stomach isn't indestructible. It's just very well defended. Tag a chilli lover 🌶️👇
