## Intent

Diwali-timed bright pixel-art science short, built autonomously from the viral-topic research.
Hook: a five-colour burst over a Diwali skyline, "Every colour is a different metal". Then into an
atom (heat kicks an electron up, it falls back and emits light), a parade of element cards
(strontium red, sodium gold, barium green, copper blue, strontium + copper purple), the twist
"Blue is the hardest to make" (copper compounds break down if the flame is too hot), finale with
diyas and rangoli, loop. Sound effects only.
