What Is a Token? 🎧

Does AI actually read your words? Nope. It reads tokens.

When you type, AI chops your message into little blocks. "Hello! How are you?" is 6 of them. A short word like "cat" is one block. Bigger words split up: jelly + fish, and Tyr + ann + os + aurus. On average one token is about ¾ of a word, so 100 tokens ≈ 75 words. Tokens decide what AI costs (it's billed per token) and how much it can hold at once (its context window). When the window is full, the oldest blocks get pushed out. To an AI, " strawberry" is one block, which is a big reason chatbots used to say it has 2 R's. It has 3.

Sources: OpenAI tiktoken tokenizer (o200k_base / cl100k_base); OpenAI Help Center, "What are tokens and how to count them?"; Anthropic & OpenAI API pricing pages (per-token billing)
