The Animal That Poops Cubes 🎧

Which animal poops cubes? The wombat.

Bare-nosed wombats make about 80–100 cube-shaped droppings every night, each around 2 cm wide. Their intestine has stiff and stretchy patches that mould the poop into corners as it dries. Wombats leave the cubes on rocks and logs to mark their territory, and scientists think the flat sides help them stay put instead of rolling away. The research won the 2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics.

Sources: Yang et al. (2021), Soft Matter 17, "Intestines of non-uniform stiffness mold the corners of wombat feces"; Improbable Research, Ig Nobel Prize winners 2019
