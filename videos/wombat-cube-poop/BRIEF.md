## Facts

Yang, Chan, Hu et al. (2021), Soft Matter 17, 475–488, "Intestines of non-uniform stiffness mold the corners of wombat feces":
bare-nosed wombats produce about 80–100 cubes a night, ~2 cm each; regions of the intestine wall with different stiffness
shape the corners. The cubes are deposited on rocks and logs to mark territory; their shape likely keeps them from rolling
away (a hypothesis). Ig Nobel Prize in Physics 2019.
