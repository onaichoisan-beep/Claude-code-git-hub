# Storyboard: "Nobody in Chennai offered it" (1-min YouTube highlight)

**Source:** 6:47 interview at Chill Breeze salon, Valluvar Kottam, Chennai (host in black top + Dr. Suman of
The Madras Cosmetic Clinic in pink). Shot on a phone held vertically but stored sideways; Tamil–English.
**Output:** 1920×1080 (16:9), 30 fps, about 66 s, YouTube.
**Style:** `video-playbook/PODCAST_EDIT_STYLE.md`: word-by-word captions (white connectors, **yellow** keyword,
**red** shock word), punch-ins to hide jump cuts, a quick SFX on every graphic, and a music bed under the voice.

## Why this minute
Of the 6:47, only 3:48–6:17 carries real value:
- **A personal, relatable problem → solution story**: she needed a lymphatic drainage facial before a film role,
  nobody in Chennai offered it, and this clinic did it. "I can see the difference."
- **The most useful info in the video**: the doctor's full treatment menu (skin, anti-ageing, acne, hair, laser, slimming).
- **A reassuring close**: "It sounds scary, but it's actually very simple, and there's a professional doctor around."

Dropped: rolling/setup chatter (0:00–0:06), intros and salon history (0:06–2:20), the "passion" tangent (2:42–3:20),
product talk about O3 (3:51–3:56), and goodbyes (6:17–6:47).

## Edit rules
- **Reframe:** rotate 90° upright → crop a 16:9 band. Three shots: **WIDE** (both women on the sofa),
  **HOST CU** (right side), **DOC CU** (left side).
- **Pauses cut:** every silence over 0.25 s inside a sentence is cut to about 0.08 s. Each cut snaps to the quietest 10 ms nearby.
  That's 33 pieces, about 63 s of speech taken from about 86 s of source.
- **Jump cuts hidden** by alternating CU ↔ WIDE on every cut inside the same speaker.
- **Captions** are English (translated from the Tamil-English speech), 1–4 words at a time, in the lower third.
