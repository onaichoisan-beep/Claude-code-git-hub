The Crows That Never Forget 🐦‍⬛🎭🎧

This man is hated by every crow in Seattle.

In 2006, scientists wore a creepy caveman mask while catching and tagging 7 crows. Years later, crows still scream at anyone in that mask, even crows that were never caught, and babies that weren't born yet. The parents taught them the face. Put the mask on an innocent person? Screamed at. Take it off? Nothing.

Be nice to crows. They're watching 👀

Sources: Marzluff et al., University of Washington; Cornell, Marzluff & Pecoraro (2010) Animal Behaviour
