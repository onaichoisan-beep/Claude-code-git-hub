This Is Not Her Baby 🐣🎧

Why is this tiny bird feeding a baby 8 times her weight?

A cuckoo sneaks one egg into a reed warbler's nest in about 10 seconds. It hatches first, and the blind newborn pushes every other egg out of the nest. So why doesn't mom notice? Its begging call sounds like a whole nest of hungry babies, so she works for four, until it flies off to Africa. Next spring, she lays four eggs again.

Who else feels used? 😅

Sources: Davies, Kilner & Noble (1998) Proc. R. Soc. B; Davies (2000)
