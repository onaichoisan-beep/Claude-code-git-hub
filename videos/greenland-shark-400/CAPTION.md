This Shark Might Be Older Than the USA 🦈🧊🎧

How old can a shark get? Maybe 400 years.

The Greenland shark lives in freezing Arctic water, swims at about walking speed and grows less than 1 cm a year. In 2016, scientists carbon-dated the lens of its eye (it forms before birth and never changes) and found the oldest one was about 392 years old, give or take 120. It was probably born in the 1600s: before Newton's laws of motion, before the USA existed, and still swimming in 2016. It only becomes an adult at about 150. The longest-living vertebrate known to science. ⏳
