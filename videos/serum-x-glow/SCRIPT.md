# Serum X: "Glow In My Own Shade" (song-ad script)

Style: `video-playbook/SONG_AD_STYLE.md` (whole video is one song, lyric
captions, stylised 3D, problem → mentor → mechanism → results → offer).
Length ~95 s · 9:16 · 30 fps · ~95 BPM · one lyric line ≈ one shot (~2.2 s).

## Creative rule: she keeps her skin tone

The heroine has **deep brown skin before and after**. The problem the serum
fixes is **dull, patchy, irritated skin and the confidence she lost**, not
her shade. Every "after" shot must show the **same skin tone**, only with
smoother texture and a healthy glow.

Why: an ad where a dark-skinned girl is rejected and then loved after a
product reads as a skin-lightening ad. That is harmful colorism. It also
breaks ad rules: Meta and Google ad policies, and India's ASCI guidelines on
skin-tone ads, which ban showing darker skin as unattractive or a handicap.
The hook line "Glow in my own shade" turns that into the brand message.

## Characters

- **Anaya** (lead, 19): deep brown skin, long curly hair, college student.
  Before: oversized grey hoodie, hair over her face, tired eyes. After: the same
  face and shade, mustard kurta-top + denim jacket, hair tied back.
- **Meera Didi** (mentor, 26): her older cousin, warm and funny, the one who
  explains the skincare. She plays the "Eddie the barber" role.
- **Classmates**: a mixed group; the same 4 faces appear before and after.

> young female vocalist, melodic half-rap verses, big catchy sung hook,

Use section tags `[Verse 1] [Hook] [Verse 2] [Hook] [Verse 3] [Outro]`. The

## On-screen elements

**Captions:** the full lyric line, ALL CAPS, white, heavy sans (Montserrat
ExtraBold) with a dark outline and soft shadow, centred at ~73% of frame
height, up to 2 lines. No box. The caption changes on each line.

**Ingredient overlays (0:44–0:53):** thin cream/gold serif name at the top
third with a hairline gold underline, plus a small grey sans benefit line under
it. Fade up over 0.3 s; never cover her face.

**End card (1:30–1:35):** a deep plum → warm gold gradient with soft floating
particles. White bold headline "GLOW IN YOUR OWN SHADE". The Serum X bottle
sits centred on a glowing pedestal. A gold pill button: "Tap Shop Now".
