## Intent

Bright pixel-art science short in the user's style, built autonomously. Hook: a single glowing dot,
"Everything you see was once this small". A timeline counter sits upper-left (time since the Big Bang,
with a progress bar). Small centred text, soft ambient sound only — explicitly NOT dramatic for the
Big Bang or star formation. Loop: Earth shrinks back into the opening dot.

Beats (standard cosmology): Big Bang, space stretches (0 s) → first hydrogen and helium nuclei (~3 min)
→ recombination, light breaks free / CMB (~380,000 yr) → first stars (~100-200 Myr) → galaxies and
supernovae forge heavier elements (~1 Gyr) → solar nebula collapses, Sun forms (~9.2 Gyr, 4.6 Gya)
→ Earth accretes (~4.54 Gya), Theia impact forms the Moon → today (13.8 Gyr).
