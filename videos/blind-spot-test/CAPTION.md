There's a Hole in Your Vision 👁️🎧

Do you have a blind spot? Everyone does. Try the test in the video.

Close your left eye, stare at the X with your right eye and slowly bring your phone closer. At about 20 cm the red dot should vanish. That's your blind spot: the place at the back of your eye where the optic nerve leaves, with no light-sensing cells at all. You never notice it because your brain fills in the gap, and with both eyes open each eye covers the other's blind spot.

Did the dot disappear for you? 👇
