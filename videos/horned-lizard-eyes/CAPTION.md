The Lizard That Cries Blood 🦎🩸🎧

Why would a lizard shoot blood out of its eyes? To get rid of coyotes.

The Texas horned lizard first tries puffing itself up. If that fails, it raises the blood pressure in its head and squirts blood from its eyes, up to about 1.5 m (5 ft). The blood contains chemicals that dogs, foxes and coyotes find disgusting, so they drop it and back off. Hawks aren't bothered at all, so against birds it relies on staying still and blending into the sand.
