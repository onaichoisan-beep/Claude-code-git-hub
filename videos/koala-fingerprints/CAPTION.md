The Koala Did It 🐨🔍🎧

Could a koala's fingerprint fool a detective? Scientists say it could be very hard to tell.

Koalas have fingerprints with loops, whorls and ridges so similar to ours that they are very difficult to tell apart, even under a microscope. They didn't get them from a shared ancestor: primates and koalas evolved fingerprints separately, probably to help grip and feel things like eucalyptus branches. (The cookie-jar crime is fiction. Koalas eat almost only eucalyptus leaves, so who ate the cookies? 👀)
