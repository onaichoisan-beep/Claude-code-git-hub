## Intent

A ~30s vertical pixel-art science short about octopus camouflage, told as a hunt:
a crab scuttles across the reef past a "rock" that turns out to be an octopus.
Camouflage facts are revealed during the ambush. Style follows
/home/user/Claude-code-git-hub/pixel-explainer-style.md exactly (the @BITWONDER-style
references): 180x320 art canvas upscaled 4x, pixel caption cards on black boxes with
dither-dissolve exit, one continuous camera take, constant ambient motion, looping ending.

Beats: hook "WHERE IS THE OCTOPUS?" over empty reef → crab enters → rock skin flickers
"SKIN WITH ~10,000 COLOUR CELLS" → bumps rise "IT CHANGES TEXTURE TOO" → strike
"IN UNDER 0.2 SECONDS" (gold number) → twist "IT'S COLOURBLIND" → loop to empty reef.
