## Intent

Timely (October 2026 news: evidence of a supermassive black hole pair spiralling toward a collision in
the blazar galaxy Markarian 501) bright pixel-art science short, built autonomously. Cold open on the
orbiting pair; story: the galaxy and its jet → two giants, each millions of suns heavy → they shake
space (gravitational waves) → faster and faster → merger and ringdown → more power (in gravitational
waves) than all the stars in the observable universe → the ripples reach Earth → LIGO's 4 km arms
stretch by less than a proton → loop. Sound: deep drone + a real-style gravitational-wave chirp.

## Notes

- Mrk 501 distance ~456 million light-years (z ≈ 0.034). Peak gravitational-wave luminosity of a
  black-hole merger exceeds the combined light of all stars in the observable universe (e.g. GW150914).
- LIGO arm change for GW150914 was ~1/1000 the width of a proton. No timeline is claimed for the merger.
