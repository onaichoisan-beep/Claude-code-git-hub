The Parasite That Becomes a Fish's Tongue 👅🐟

What's inside this fish's mouth? Not its tongue.

The tongue-eating louse (Cymothoa exigua) swims into a fish through its gills as a male, then turns female inside. It grabs the tongue and drinks its blood until the tongue withers and falls off. Then it clamps onto the stump and becomes the fish's new tongue. The fish keeps eating normally, with a parasite in its mouth for the rest of its life. It's the only parasite known to functionally replace a whole organ of its host.
