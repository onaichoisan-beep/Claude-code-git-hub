Facts: Pearlfish (Carapidae) enter holothurians tail-first via the cloaca; Encheliophis species are parasitic and eat host
gonads (Parmentier & Vandewalle 2005; Parmentier & Das 2004). Holothurians ventilate respiratory trees through the cloaca.
"Anal teeth" (e.g. Actinopyga) are thought to deter pearlfish. Evisceration + regeneration over weeks (García-Arrarás &
Greenberg 2001). Cartoon licence: the sea cucumber has eyes and a face.
