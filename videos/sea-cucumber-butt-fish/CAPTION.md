The Fish That Lives in a Sea Cucumber's Butt 🐟🍑

Where does a pearlfish live? Inside a sea cucumber's butt.

Sea cucumbers breathe through their butt: they pump water in and out of it all day long. So the pearlfish waits for it to open and slides in, tail first. Some pearlfish even nibble on their host's insides. So some sea cucumbers have "butt teeth," which probably help keep them out. And when a predator attacks, many sea cucumbers can shoot their own guts out of their body as a decoy… then grow them back within weeks.
