One Inch Every Night 🏠🔑

Something in the Tanaka house moved one inch every night. Who was doing it?

The kotatsu. The shoe shelf. The TV. Just one inch a night, so nobody noticed… until the table was blocking the garden door. Dad scattered flour on the floor, and the next morning there were footprints from the front door. Someone had a key. By day 30 the family sold the house for half the price. The buyer? The man who sold it to them in the first place. 😳

Would you have caught him? 👇
