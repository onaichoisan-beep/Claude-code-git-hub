Bees Cook a Giant Hornet Alive 🐝🔥🎧

How do tiny bees beat a giant hornet? They cook it.

A single Asian giant hornet can kill dozens of honeybees a minute, and their stings can't get through its armour. But Japanese honeybees have a trick: about 500 of them swarm the hornet into a tight ball and shiver their flight muscles. The ball heats up to around 46°C. Hornets die at about 44–46°C, but the bees can survive up to 48–50°C. They win by just a few degrees. 🌡️
