The Beetle That Uses the Galaxy 🪲🌌🎧

This beetle is steering by the Milky Way. While pushing poop.

Dung beetles roll their ball in a straight line so other beetles can't steal it. First they climb on top and spin, taking a "photo" of the sky. On moonless nights, scientists tested them in a planetarium: stars on, straight lines; stars off, circles; just the Milky Way, straight again. With tiny hats on, lost. The first animal ever shown to navigate by our galaxy.
