## Intent

The fourth of four narrated fable shorts: a dark fairy tale that lands on a real fact (leap seconds).
The theft is fiction; the 27 leap seconds since 1972 are real.

## Facts

- 8 billion seconds ≈ 253.5 years (8×10⁹ ÷ 31,557,600 s per year); the narration rounds to "250 years".
- 27 leap seconds were added to UTC between 1972 and the end of 2016; none since (IERS Bulletin C).
  They keep atomic time in step with Earth's slightly irregular rotation. In 2022 the CGPM voted to stop
  adding them by 2035.
- The clockmaker and the theft are fiction.
