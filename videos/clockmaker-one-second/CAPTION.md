The Clockmaker Who Stole One Second 🎧

Every person alive is missing one second of their life. A fable about who took it.

In a city of clocks, a clockmaker's daughter was dying. So he built a clock that could steal time: one second from every person on Earth. 8 billion seconds is about 250 years of life, and she saw every spring after that. But a thief always pays. Since 1972 the world's clocks have added 27 extra "leap seconds" (yes, clocks really have shown 23:59:60). Scientists say they keep atomic time in step with Earth's wobbly spin. Some say he's paying it back.

The clockmaker is fiction; the leap seconds are real.

Sources: IERS Bulletin C (leap seconds 1972–2016); NIST Time and Frequency Division; BIPM / CGPM 2022 resolution on the leap second
