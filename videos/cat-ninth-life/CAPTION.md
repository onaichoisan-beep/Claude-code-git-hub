The Cat on Its 9th Life 🎧

What happened to the other eight lives? Mittens tells you himself.

Egypt worshipped him (the crocodile didn't). A Viking ship, a fish, overboard. A nap in a fireworks shop. The captain's parrot. The top of the Eiffel Tower, and forgetting about down. A rocket to space (no fish). A scientist's box (long story). And last Tuesday… a cucumber. Now he's on his last life and being very, very careful. Mostly.
