Orcas vs Great White Sharks 🐋🦈🎧

What scares a great white shark? Orcas.

Off South Africa, two orcas named Port and Starboard started hunting great white sharks. Working together, they flip the shark upside down, which makes it go limp (it's called tonic immobility). Then they take only the liver, which is huge and packed with energy-rich oil. After the attacks, the other great whites fled the area, and some stayed away for months. The ocean's "top predator" has a predator.
