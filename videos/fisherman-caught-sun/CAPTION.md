The Fisherman Who Caught the Sun 🎧

Why do we light diyas? An old fisherman once caught the Sun in his net… (a fable)

One evening, old Raghu's net came up burning. He hid the Sun in his hut, and morning never came. The sea froze, the birds went silent, and the whole village shivered in the dark until he rowed out and set the Sun free. But first, he broke off one tiny crumb. That's why every little diya flame holds a crumb of the Sun.

An original fable, not a real myth. Diyas are the clay oil lamps lit for Diwali, the festival of lights.
