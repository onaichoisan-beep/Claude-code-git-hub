How Many Descendants Will YOU Have? 🧬 (Part 3)

You needed 8.5 billion ancestors to exist. But how many people will need you?

Have 2 kids. They each have 2. Every generation, it doubles. In 300 years: about 1,000 descendants. In 600 years: a million. In 1,000 years: over 8.5 billion, more than everyone alive today. Here's the crazy part: scientists who modelled family trees found there's no middle ground. Go far enough into the future and either your family line dies out completely… or you become an ancestor of every single person alive.

Nothing in between. Comment 0 or ALL 👇

Sources: Chang (1999), "Recent common ancestors of all present-day individuals", Advances in Applied Probability; Rohde, Olson & Chang (2004), "Modelling the recent common ancestry of all living humans", Nature
