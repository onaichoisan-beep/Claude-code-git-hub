Why You Can't Tickle Yourself 🤭🧠🎧

Why doesn't it tickle when you do it? Your brain sees it coming.

The cerebellum, at the back of your brain, predicts what your own movements will feel like and turns down the expected touch. Tickling needs surprise, so when a friend does it, it works. Scientists tested this with a robot: people moved a joystick and a robot touched their palm. Adding a delay of about 0.1 to 0.3 seconds made their own touch feel more ticklish, because the brain's prediction no longer matched.
