A Bucket of Slime in One Second 🐟🟢🎧

What happens when a shark bites a hagfish? It instantly regrets it.

When a hagfish is attacked, glands along its body release slime that expands up to about 10,000 times in seawater in a fraction of a second. It clogs the predator's gills, and sharks have been filmed spitting it out and swimming away. To clean up, the hagfish ties its body into a knot and slides it down to scrape the slime off. In July 2017 a truck carrying about 3,400 kg (7,500 lb) of hagfish spilled on Highway 101 in Oregon and slimed the road and a car.
