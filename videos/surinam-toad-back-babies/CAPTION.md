Babies Hatch Out of Her BACK 🐸🕳️

What's in the holes on this toad's back? Her babies.

The Surinam toad lives in South American rivers and is as flat as a leaf. When a pair mates, they do somersaults underwater and up to around 100 eggs land on the female's back. Her skin swells up and swallows every egg into its own little pocket. For about 3 to 4 months the babies grow inside her skin, skipping the tadpole stage completely. Then fully formed baby toads push their way out, one by one. Afterwards she sheds that skin and her back is smooth again.
