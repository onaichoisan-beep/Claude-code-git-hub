Topic: what a rattlesnake bite does inside the body (vertical pixel-art science short, ~30s, Onyx narration).
Facts: strike is faster than a blink (well under 0.1 s); hollow fangs inject venom like hypodermic needles;
pit-viper venom is mostly hemotoxic — it stops blood from clotting and breaks down tissue; antivenom is made by
immunising horses (or sheep) with small venom doses and collecting their antibodies, which bind and neutralise venom.
Safety: a bite is a medical emergency — get to a hospital; don't cut, suck or tourniquet.
