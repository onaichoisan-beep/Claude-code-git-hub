## Intent

A ~30s vertical pixel-art science short, same style as videos/octopus-camouflage-hunt
(180x320 art canvas upscaled 6x, pixel caption cards with dither-dissolve exit, one
continuous camera take, constant ambient motion, looping ending), plus dramatic Onyx
narration reading each caption and loud, clearly audible SFX.

Story: "Venom inside you". A rattlesnake strikes → slow-mo hollow fangs inject venom →
camera dives into the bloodstream where venom stops clotting and destroys tissue →
twist: antivenom antibodies (made in horses) flood in and grab the venom → loop.

## Customizations

- Narration: Kokoro am_onyx, speed ~0.9, one line per caption.
- SFX: loud and dramatic (rattle, strike, heartbeat, impacts), ducked under the voice.
- Responsible close: a short "get to a hospital" line; no gore.

## Notes

- Facts: rattlesnake strike lasts well under a tenth of a second; fangs are hollow and work
  like hypodermic needles; pit-viper venom is largely hemotoxic (disrupts clotting, damages
  tissue); antivenom is made by immunising animals — horses (e.g. Anavip) or sheep (CroFab).
