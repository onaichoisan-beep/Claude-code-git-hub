Where Did Your First 3 Years Go? 👶🧠🎧

You lived a thousand days you don't remember.

It's called infantile amnesia, and most people's earliest memory is from around age 3. But in 2025, scientists scanned babies' brains while showing them pictures, and found that from about one year old, babies are already saving memories. So maybe they're not deleted, just locked. In mice, scientists have even switched lost baby memories back on with light.
