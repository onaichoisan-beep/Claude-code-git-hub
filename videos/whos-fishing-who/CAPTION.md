Who's Fishing Who? 🎧

Ever wondered who's on the other end of the line? Everybody. Everybody is.

A sleepy fisherman bites a burger that rises out of the sea... and gets reeled in by a fish in a bucket hat. That fish bites a worm and gets reeled in by a bigger fish. That one bites a lure and meets a giant anglerfish... who bites a worm on the fisherman's own rod. Four fishermen, one ring, zero winners. Watch it twice: the end is the beginning.
