₹1 Crore or ₹1 Doubled for 30 Days? 💰🎧

Which would you pick? Most people pick the crore… and lose.

Start with ₹1 and double it every day. Day 10: just ₹512, not even a pizza. Day 20: ₹5.2 lakh, still losing. But on day 25 it hits ₹1.67 crore and overtakes. And on day 30? ₹53,68,70,912. That's over 53 crore rupees from a single rupee. That's the power of doubling (and of compound growth).

Comment 1 (₹1 crore) or 2 (₹1 doubled) 👇

Maths: day n = 2^(n−1) rupees, so day 30 = 2^29 = ₹53,68,70,912
