The Beetle That Escapes From Inside a Toad 🪲🐸🎧

Can you survive being eaten? This beetle can.

When a toad swallows a bombardier beetle, the beetle doesn't give up. Inside the toad's stomach it fires a boiling, 100°C chemical spray from its rear, in rapid pulses about 500 times a second. Again and again. The toad gets sick and throws it back up, and the beetle walks away alive, sometimes after up to 107 minutes inside. In a 2018 study, 43% of the toads vomited the beetles back out.
