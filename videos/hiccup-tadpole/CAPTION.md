Hiccups Are a Fish Thing 🐸🎧

Why do we hiccup? One theory says it's left over from tadpoles.

A hiccup is a sudden spasm of your diaphragm that pulls air in, and then your glottis (the gate in your throat) snaps shut. That's the "hic". Tadpoles do almost the same thing to breathe through their gills: they gulp water and close the glottis so the water leaves over the gills. Some scientists think hiccups may be that old reflex we never lost. The record: Charles Osborne hiccupped for 68 years, from 1922 to 1990, an estimated 430 million times, and still had 8 children.
