You Glow in the Dark 🎧

Do humans really glow? Yes, just too faintly to see.

Chemical reactions from your metabolism give off tiny amounts of visible light. In 2009, Japanese scientists filmed people in total darkness with ultra-sensitive cooled cameras and caught the glow. It's about 1,000 times too faint for your eyes, it's brightest on the face (especially the cheeks), and it rises through the day: dimmest around 10 am and brightest around 4 pm.

Sources: Kobayashi, Kikuchi & Okamura (2009), PLoS ONE 4(7): e6256, "Imaging of ultraweak spontaneous photon emission from human body displaying diurnal rhythm"
