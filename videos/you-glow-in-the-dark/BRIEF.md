## Facts

Kobayashi, Kikuchi & Okamura (2009), PLoS ONE 4(7): e6256, "Imaging of ultraweak spontaneous photon emission from human body
displaying diurnal rhythm": five healthy men filmed with cooled CCD cameras in total darkness; emission ~1,000× below what the
naked eye can see; the face glowed most; lowest around 10 am, highest around 4 pm. The light comes from metabolism-related
(free-radical) reactions.
