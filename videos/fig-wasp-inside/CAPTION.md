There's a Wasp in This Fig 🐝🟣

Have you eaten a wasp? If you've eaten figs, maybe.

A fig is actually hundreds of tiny flowers growing inside-out, hidden in a pod. Only one insect can reach them, a 2 mm fig wasp. She squeezes through a tiny hole at the bottom so tight that her wings rip off, pollinates the flowers, and can never get out again. She dies inside, and the fig's enzyme (ficin) breaks her down. That crunch? Seeds, not wasp legs. (Many supermarket figs ripen without any wasp at all.)
