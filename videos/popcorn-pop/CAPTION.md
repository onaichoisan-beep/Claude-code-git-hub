Why Popcorn Pops (and JUMPS) 🍿🎧

Why does popcorn pop? Steam.

Every kernel hides a tiny drop of water. Heat it and the water turns to steam, but the tough shell won't let it out. The pressure climbs to about 9 times the air around you, and at around 180°C the shell bursts. The soft starch inside puffs into foam up to 40 times bigger. In slow motion, scientists saw something wild: the popcorn grows a little starch "leg" that kicks off the pan and makes it jump. And the "pop" sound? That's the steam escaping. The duds at the bottom? Their shells were cracked, so the steam leaked out.
