# Pixy "Dr. Dragon" — official character (final)

A tiny doctor in a dragon hood: mascot of **Scroll 2 Score** (NEET / MBBS reels).

## Look
- **Hood:** blue dragon hood, jaw framing the face, small rounded fangs (3 top, 2 bottom), angry-cute dragon eyes, gold horns, orange crest spikes, scale marks.
- **Face:** big expressive eyes (white disc + large pupil, eyelids for attitude), blush, tiny mouths.
- **Body:** Chi-style proportions — small torso, long noodle arms with mitten hands, long legs, big round shoes.
- **Outfit:** cream lab coat, blue shirt, stethoscope, "PIXY" badge with orange stripe, brown trousers, blue shoes.
