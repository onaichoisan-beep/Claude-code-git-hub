Hotter Than the Sun 🦐💥🎧

Can a shrimp make something almost as hot as the Sun? Yes, for a split second.

The pistol shrimp's big claw can be up to half its body length. It snaps shut so fast that the water jet creates a bubble, and when the bubble collapses it reaches an estimated 4,700 °C (the Sun's surface is about 5,500 °C). It even gives off a tiny flash of light and a bang loud enough to stun small fish. In World War II, colonies of snapping shrimp made so much noise that they cluttered navy sonar, and submarines used them to hide.
