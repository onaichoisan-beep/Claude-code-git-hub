The Frog That Freezes Solid 🐸🧊🎧

Can a frog die and come back? The wood frog basically does, every winter.

As it gets cold, the wood frog floods its cells with sugar as antifreeze. Then up to about two-thirds of its body water turns to ice. Its heart stops and it stops breathing. In Alaska, wood frogs have survived temperatures around -18 °C and up to about 218 days frozen, roughly 7 months. In spring it thaws, the heart starts beating again and it hops away. Scientists study it for clues to freezing human organs for transplants.
