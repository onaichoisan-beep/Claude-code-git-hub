Story: hook (two strike-anywhere tips rubbed → FWOOSH) → rewind → two normal safety matches rubbed:
usually nothing → inside the head: potassium chlorate (oxygen) + sulfur (fuel) + glass + glue, but no
spark → the box strip: red phosphorus + ground glass → slow-mo strike: friction heat turns a speck of
red P into white P, which ignites in air → chlorate releases oxygen, sulfur erupts → wax-soaked wood

they're called safety matches" → don't try this at home → loop.
