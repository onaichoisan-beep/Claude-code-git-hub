## Intent

Fast, cinematic pixel-art mythology short in the user's style: cold-open hook (the sun swallowed,
"A baby ate the sun") then a rewind glitch into the story; few words, small and centred; cinematic
sound effects only; loop ending (back to the sun before the gulp).

Story: hungry baby Hanuman sees the rising sun as a mango → flies at it → swallows it, the world goes dark →
Indra's vajra strikes his jaw → the wind god Vayu, his father, stops all air → the gods heal him and
grant boons → "Hanu means jaw — so he was named Hanuman."
