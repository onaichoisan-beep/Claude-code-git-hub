How Many People Did It Take to Make You? 🎧

How many ancestors do you have? More than have ever been alive at once.

2 parents, 4 grandparents, 8 great-grandparents… it doubles every generation. Go back 33 generations, to around the year 1036, and your family tree needs 8,589,934,592 people. But only about 300 million people were alive on Earth back then. The answer: your tree folds in on itself. Distant cousins had children together, so the same ancestor fills thousands of spots in your tree. Go back a few thousand years and, according to genealogy models, everyone alive today shares the same ancestors. Hello, cousin 👋
