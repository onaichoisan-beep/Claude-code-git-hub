Half of You Isn't You 🎧

Are you more bacteria than human? By count, roughly yes.

A typical adult has about 30 trillion human cells and about 38 trillion bacteria. Most of the bacteria live in your gut, where they break down fibre you can't digest, make vitamins like K and some B vitamins, and help train your immune system. All of them together weigh only about 0.2 kg, about the same as one apple.

Sources: Sender, Fuchs & Milo (2016), PLoS Biology, "Revised estimates for the number of human and bacteria cells in the body"; LeBlanc et al. (2013), Current Opinion in Biotechnology (vitamins from gut bacteria)
