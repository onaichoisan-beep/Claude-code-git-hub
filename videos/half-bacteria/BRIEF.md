## Facts

Sender, Fuchs & Milo (2016), PLoS Biology, "Revised estimates for the number of human and bacteria cells in the body":
~3.0×10^13 human cells, ~3.8×10^13 bacteria (ratio ~1.3:1), bacteria mass ~0.2 kg, mostly in the colon.
Gut bacteria ferment fibre we can't digest, make vitamin K and several B vitamins, and shape the immune system.
