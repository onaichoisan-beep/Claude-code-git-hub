Raindrop Race 🎧

Ever watched two raindrops race down a window? They were racing. For real.

Lights out on a storm cloud, a skydive through the city, SPLAT onto the windscreen, then a race to the bottom of the glass. Swallow a smaller drop and you get faster (that part is real: merged drops run faster down glass). Watch out for the wiper. The winner gets a podium, a trophy... and the sun. Winner's prize? Another race.
