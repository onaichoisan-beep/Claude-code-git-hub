Your Brain Doesn't Care If You're Happy 🧠🎧

Why does one bad comment beat 99 nice ones? Negativity bias.

Your brain's first job is keeping you alive. So it pays extra attention to anything that might be a threat: you replay the embarrassing moment and forget the compliment, one negative comment can sour a whole day, and you keep preparing for things that might go wrong. Psychologists call it negativity bias, and the usual explanation is evolutionary: for our ancestors, missing one predator in the bushes could cost their life, while missing a nice moment cost nothing. Your brain still runs the same alarm for a comment that it ran for a tiger.

So remember: not every negative thought deserves your attention. Send this to an overthinker 👇

Sources: Baumeister et al. (2001) "Bad Is Stronger Than Good", Review of General Psychology; Rozin & Royzman (2001) Personality and Social Psychology Review; Vaish, Grossmann & Woodward (2008) Psychological Bulletin
