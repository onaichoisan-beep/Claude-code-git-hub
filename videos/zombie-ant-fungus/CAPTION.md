The Zombie Ant 🐜🍄🎧

Can a fungus control an ant? Yes, and it's terrifying.

When a spore of the Ophiocordyceps fungus lands on a carpenter ant, the fungus grows through its body and takes over its muscles (the brain is left alone). The ant leaves its colony, climbs a plant to about 25 cm high, and around noon bites into a leaf and locks its jaw. Then it dies there. A stalk grows out of its head and rains spores onto the ants below, and it starts all over again. 🧟

Sources: Hughes et al. (2011), BMC Ecology 11:13; Andersen et al. (2009), The American Naturalist 174; Fredericksen et al. (2017), PNAS 114
