Your Brain Isn't Freezing 🍦🧠🎧

Why does ice cream hurt your forehead?

It's not your brain freezing. Cold hits the roof of your mouth, the blood vessels there widen fast, and a nerve sends a pain signal... but your brain gets the location wrong and feels it in your forehead. Doctors call it referred pain. The fix: press your tongue to the roof of your mouth to warm it up. Pain gone in seconds.

Save this for summer ☀️

Sources: Serrador et al. (2012) FASEB Journal
