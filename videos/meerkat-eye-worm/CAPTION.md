The Bird That Pulled a Worm From a Meerkat's Eye 🎧

Can a bird really pull a worm out of a meerkat's eye? Eye worms are real — but not like that.

That viral clip shows a fat, segmented grub yanked from a meerkat's eye. Real eye worms (Thelazia) live on the
surface of the eye, are spread by flies sipping tears, and are thread-thin — about 0.5 to 2 cm long. A grub that
size? Probably AI.

Sources: CDC DPDx – Thelaziasis; Otranto & Traversa, "Thelazia eyeworm", Trends in Parasitology (2005)
