Ant Everest 🎧

What's the deadliest climb on Earth? It depends how big you are.

They said no ant could climb it: 16 mm of sheer crystal. Avalanches of sugar grains, blizzards from a salt shaker, three days without a single crumb. And at last, he planted the flag on the summit... and someone said "ooh, can you pass the sugar?" Legends never quit. There's a whole bowl of mountains left.
