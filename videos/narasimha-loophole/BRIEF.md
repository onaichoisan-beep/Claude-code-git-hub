## Intent

Fast, cinematic pixel-art mythology short in the user's style: cold-open hook (Narasimha's roar,
"No man or beast could kill him...") then a rewind glitch into the story; few words of text, small and
centred; cinematic sound effects only (impacts, brass hits, taiko, conch, gong, bells, roar); loop ending.

Story (Bhagavata Purana): the boon's five conditions → "Where is your god?" → "In this pillar." →
the pillar bursts → Narasimha, half man half lion → dusk, doorstep, on his lap, with claws → "Every rule. Broken."
→ Narasimha blesses Prahlad → loop back to the roar. Violence is implied by a white flash, never shown.
