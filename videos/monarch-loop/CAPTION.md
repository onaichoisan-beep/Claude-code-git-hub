Egg → Butterfly → Egg 🦋🔁

How does a caterpillar become a butterfly? It melts itself into soup first.

A monarch hatches after about 4 days and eats its own eggshell. Then it eats milkweed nonstop and gets about 2,000 times heavier in two weeks. It hangs upside down in a J and sheds its skin one last time into a chrysalis. Inside, it digests most of its own body into soup. Only a few tiny groups of cells (imaginal discs) survive, and they build a brand-new body: wings, legs, antennae. About 10 days later a butterfly climbs out, pumps its wings open, and later lays a single egg on milkweed. And it all starts again.
