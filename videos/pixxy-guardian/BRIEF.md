# PIXXY — The Guardian (19.2s)

**Message:** a pixel guardian spirit, PIXXY, quietly saves people from everyday near-misses on a rainy night.

An original pixel-art brand spot. It borrows only the cut rhythm and the broad "guardian saves people" idea from
a reference clip the owner supplied. The character, scenes, art and sound are all new.
