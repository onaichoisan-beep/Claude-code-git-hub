How Many Humans Have EVER Lived? 👥🎧 (Part 2)

8 billion people are alive right now. How many have ever lived? About 117 billion.

Scientists at the Population Reference Bureau counted back about 200,000 years. For most of history there were only a few million people, but babies kept being born, generation after generation: hunters, farmers, kings and every one of your ancestors. Their best estimate is around 117 billion humans ever born. That means for every person alive today, about 14 have lived and died before. Only about 7% of all humans who ever lived are alive right now. You're in the 7%.
