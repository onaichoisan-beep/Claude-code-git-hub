The Bird That Copies a Chainsaw 🐦🪚🎧

Can a bird really sound like a chainsaw? The lyrebird can.

The superb lyrebird from Australia copies the songs of other birds almost perfectly: kookaburras, whipbirds and many more. Some lyrebirds, especially ones living near people, have even copied camera shutters, car alarms and chainsaws. Why? To impress females: the male fans his lyre-shaped tail over his head and sings and dances at the same time. 🔊
