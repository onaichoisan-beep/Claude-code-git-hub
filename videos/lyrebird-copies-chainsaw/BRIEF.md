synthesised in scripts/synth_audio.py and plays exactly while the beak is open (MIMIC list shared via window.BEATS), with a
thought cloud showing what it copies and the SOUNDS COPIED counter ticking; at SHOWTIME the tail fans over the head and the
copies land on the beat of an 8-bit groove, faster and faster (counter 20+). The female arrives, "IMPRESSED? YES".
Facts: Australian Museum; Dalziell et al. (2013) Current Biology 23: 1132; human-made sound mimicry is documented mainly in
captive or urban-adjacent birds, hence "SOME HAVE COPIED".
