## Intent

Bright pixel-art science short in the user's established style (see videos/snake-bite-venom):
one continuous camera journey, only key facts as small text in the middle, sound effects only,
complete story start to finish, looping ending. Built autonomously.

Story: hand gets its pinky pulled — POP → joint cut-away filled with synovial fluid → pulling
stretches the capsule, pressure drops → gas forms a bubble (the pop) → ~20 minutes before it can
crack again → one doctor cracked one hand for 60 years → no arthritis in either hand →
"Verdict: harmless. Pain? See a doctor." → loop.

## Notes

- 2015 real-time MRI study (Kawchuk et al.) showed the crack coincides with cavity (bubble) formation.
- Dr Donald Unger cracked only his left hand's knuckles for ~60 years; no arthritis in either hand (Ig Nobel 2009).
- ~20-minute refractory period is the commonly cited figure.
