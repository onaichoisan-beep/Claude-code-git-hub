The Animal That Survived Space 🐻🚀🎧

Can anything live in open space with no suit? Tardigrades can.

Tardigrades ("water bears") are about 0.5 mm long. When they dry out they curl into a "tun" and almost stop living. In 2007, scientists sent dried tardigrades into orbit on the FOTON-M3 mission and opened the box to space for 10 days: no air, freezing cold, cosmic radiation. Back on Earth they added water, many woke up, and they went on to lay eggs that hatched. (Full, unfiltered sunlight was too much for most of them, though.) In tun form they have also survived about -272°C, around 6,000 atmospheres of pressure, and 30 years frozen in the lab.

In 2019 the Beresheet lander crashed on the Moon carrying dried tardigrades. A 2021 study says they probably didn't survive the impact. What do you think: yes or no? 👇

Sources: Jönsson et al. (2008) Current Biology; Seki & Toyoshima (1998) Nature; Tsujimoto et al. (2016) Cryobiology; Traspas & Burchell (2021) Astrobiology
